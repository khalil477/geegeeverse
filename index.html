How to play

Move around with WASD or arrow keys. Drag to rotate your camera; scroll to zoom. Press C to recenter the camera behind your character. Walk near a building door and press E to enter, or use the directory on the left. On mobile, use the direction pad. If you spawn inside a building or cannot move, click Unstuck (or press R).

Inside a classroom, interact with a practice problem on the big board, submit a hint to the shared wall, and chat with classmates. For voice, each participant must explicitly enable their microphone.

Hold Shift to sprint, Space to jump, F to talk to an NPC professor near the board, and E to enter a nearby classroom. Use the Learn tab for guided solo quests.

Building centres use real coordinates; live building and walkway footprints use City of Ottawa geodata when available. Heights, facades, trees, interiors, and classroom portals are simplified or fictional, not a true 1:1 replica.
